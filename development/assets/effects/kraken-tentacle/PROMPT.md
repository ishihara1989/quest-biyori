One attackable dark teal kraken TENTACLE emerging vertically from a tiny sand spray rim, root at (50,84), curled tip leaning LEFT, turquoise ridges and cream suckers. Four braced swaying loop frames, no mantle or heads, no extra tentacles, no terrain beyond tiny rim. Genuine transparent alpha including all interior gaps, NO checkerboard, black backdrop, haze, characters, hands, weapons, lettering or labels.
