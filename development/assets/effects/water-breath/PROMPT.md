# 水流ブレス

Built-in image_gen

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent pixel-art effect spritesheet for a side-view fantasy RPG. Exactly FOUR chronological isolated frames in 2x2 equal cells TL,TR,BL,BR, at least 12% transparent margin per cell, same pixel size, palette and origin. Crisp polished 16-bit pixel clusters. Huge LEFTward water breath cone, source RIGHT (84,50), endpoint LEFT (12,50). Frame1 small jet, frame2 widening water stream, frame3 enormous turquoise and white turbulent water cone with separated droplets and transparent gaps, frame4 spray disperses. No kraken or scenery. Genuine transparent alpha including all interior gaps, NO checkerboard, black backdrop, haze, characters, hands, weapons, lettering or labels. The emerging body-part assets contain only the explicitly described tail/tentacle.
