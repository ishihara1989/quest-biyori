Entire sprite INCLUDING smaller flowers and curled vine fully inside each cell with generous transparent margin. SAME identity, anatomy, petal shapes, flower count, pixel scale, colors, camera distance throughout. Plant contact baseline near local y=475; main flower center near local x=420. Standing full height about 390px, never fill entire cell. No labels.
Animation: seamless IDLE BREATHING loop. Frame1 neutral, arms relaxed at flower rim, main flower open. Frame2 torso lifts a little with breath, hair/leaf tips sway backward, small flower stems gently lean up. Frame3 torso settles softly, petals subtly flex downward, small stems sway forward. Frame4 near neutral recovering pose; connect naturally 4 to 1 without abrupt pose snap. Four subtly distinct frames, not identical copies.
Style: crisp hand-crafted detailed pixel art, hard square pixel clusters, stepped silhouettes, dark colored outlines, compact limited-color shading. Match reference's game asset detail. Absolutely TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA background and gaps between stems/petals. Only character pixels, no floor, cast shadow, landscape, glow, colored haze, blurred aura, checkerboard pattern, text, border, watermark or other characters. Keep entire body and every vine uncut.
