# Boss Slime animation prompts

組み込み `image_gen` を使用。既存の青い通常スライムを画風・デザイン参照にし、待機原画を全動作の同一個体参照にした。全動作4コマ、左向き、透過背景。原画は変更せず `source/` に保存。

## Idle

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent pixel-art RPG enemy sprite sheet, four IDLE animation frames.
Input image 1 is a character and style reference: the existing small blue slime sprite sheet. Create its larger BOSS form, retaining its same plain design, deep navy stepped contour, cyan and royal-blue gelatin, white upper-left pixel highlights, two short black vertical eyes on the left/front of the body, and a small tapered rear edge on the right. No crown, accessories, limbs, mouth, extra faces, or markings. Boss is bulky, about three times the normal slime's body width and height in the game, with a wide heavy rounded mound.
Make EXACTLY FOUR poses on a clean 2-column by 2-row sheet, reading top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Each cell 512x512 in a 1024x1024 image. All pose bases at local y=416 and body centers at x=256. Neutral body width about 380px and height about 252px. Empty padding around every complete sprite. Same character size, face, lighting, perspective and pixel density across all frames. True RGBA transparency everywhere outside the character, no drawn background or shadow.
Left-facing three-quarter side view matching the reference. Pixel-art discrete color clusters, crisp staircase edges, limited blue palette, no blurry anti-aliasing or gradients.
Frames: 1 neutral rounded resting mound; 2 slow inhale, body 5% taller and 3% narrower, eyes move naturally with the body; 3 relaxed slight squash, 5% lower and 4% wider; 4 rising halfway from squash to neutral, very close to frame 1, so 4-to-1 closes the breathing loop smoothly. Each is a distinct coherent pose; all stay grounded, do not hop.
No text, labels, numbers, dividers, cell lines, decorations, floor, background, motion trails, watermarks.
