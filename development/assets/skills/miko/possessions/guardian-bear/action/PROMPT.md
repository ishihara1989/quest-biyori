Reference image is locked pixel shading and palette guidance only unless identified as SAME SUBJECT. Deliberate clean square pixel clusters, dark crisp colored outlines, ivory highlights, violet-grey shadows, vermilion/white/gold shrine accents; no smooth illustration, painted gradients, blur or 3D. Real transparent RGBA, no painted checkerboard, floor, cast shadow, text, border, labels, separators or grid. All frame silhouettes fully inside x=.10..90,y=.10..90 with wide EMPTY transparent gutters. Same scale, camera, anatomy and palette across cells. Reference is SAME SUBJECT identity. Exactly THREE frames in ONE horizontal row of equal square cells. Subject: ONE full-body translucent ivory/amber sacred bear guardian, broad shoulders, TWO round ears, gold forehead knot, vermilion neck rope, TWO large forearms and paws, transparent central belly opening for host; gold shield crest on palms. Preserve identity, camera, scale and local ground/hover anchor. Position head (.50,.25), paws (.25,.58)/(.75,.58), rear feet on .84. Ordered motion: frame1 arms open wide; frame2  arms overlap in front with palms facing outward; frame3  arms settle into embracing guard. No projectiles or recipient human baked into atlas; effect is composed separately. Each whole guardian and ALL ribbons and particles remain within the central 70% of its cell, never touch an outer edge. Do not grow beyond cell gutters. The human host is composed separately.
