Real transparent RGBA, no painted checkerboard, floor, cast shadow, text, border, labels, separators or grid. All frame silhouettes fully inside x=.10..90,y=.10..90 with wide EMPTY transparent gutters. Same scale, camera, anatomy and palette across cells. Reference is SAME SUBJECT. Exactly THREE frames one row, fixed anchor .50,.86. ONE translucent ivory/jade sacred serpent with ONE head, TWO gold whiskers and no legs or wings, gold forehead jewel and vermilion neck ribbon; exactly THREE open spiral coils forming a tall enclosure, empty transparent central channel for host. Frame1 fully formed guardian in idle pose; frame2 outer arms/tail/coils separate into large ivory/red/gold ribbons keeping recognizable shape; frame3 sparse large matching ribbon fragments drifting upward. Preserve shape landmarks and frame registration. No person. Each whole guardian and ALL ribbons and particles remain within the central 70% of its cell, never touch an outer edge. Do not grow beyond cell gutters. The human host is composed separately.
