Deliberate clean square pixel clusters, dark crisp colored outlines, ivory highlights, violet-grey shadows, vermilion/white/gold shrine accents; no smooth illustration, painted gradients, blur or 3D. Real transparent RGBA, no painted checkerboard, floor, cast shadow, text, border, labels, separators or grid. All frame silhouettes fully inside x=.10..90,y=.10..90 with wide EMPTY transparent gutters. Same scale, camera, anatomy and palette across cells. Reference is SAME SUBJECT identity. Exactly THREE frames in ONE horizontal row of equal square cells. Subject: ONE actual adult white DEER, long elegant neck, narrow elongated herbivore face, horizontal deer ears, FOUR long thin legs ending in split dark hooves, tiny SHORT deer tail, gentle amber eyes, TWO branching pale-jade antlers with exactly THREE branch tips per antler, gold neck rope with red tassel; never fox face, pointed fox ears, paws or bushy tail. Preserve identity, camera, scale and local ground/hover anchor. Position head (.65,.38), antler crown (.62,.17), four hooves on .82. Ordered motion: frame1 head and antlers lower; frame2  antlers lift high; frame3  front hoof stamps as antler flowers open. No projectiles or recipient human baked into atlas; effect is composed separately. Keep all anatomy and motion effects entirely within each cell with clear 10% empty margins.
