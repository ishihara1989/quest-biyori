# caltrop-scatter

Built-in image_gen; generated RGBA preserved; nearest-neighbor packaging.

Use case: illustration-story. Production PIXEL ART ninja combat VFX RGBA transparent PNG sprite strip. One horizontal row of equal SQUARE cells, three or four as specified. No background, no text, no annotations, no arrows, no ground plane, no character. Crisp coarse pixel clusters, restrained black/steel/crimson/indigo/white/gold palette. Each whole effect has at least 12% clear transparent margin within its own cell. Animation left to right. THREE frames: 1 small cluster of black steel four-point caltrops with crimson tiny tips thrown upward in a fan, 2 caltrops falling apart toward ground in a shallow arc, 3 a spread of sharp black steel caltrops resting in a small isometric oval formation with a few tiny red perimeter dots. No colored floor fill.
