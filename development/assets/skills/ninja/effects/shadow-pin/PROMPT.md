# shadow-pin

Built-in image_gen; generated RGBA preserved; nearest-neighbor packaging.

Use case: illustration-story. Production PIXEL ART ninja combat VFX RGBA transparent PNG sprite strip. One horizontal row of equal SQUARE cells, three or four as specified. No background, no text, no annotations, no arrows, no ground plane, no character. Crisp coarse pixel clusters, restrained black/steel/crimson/indigo/white/gold palette. Each whole effect has at least 12% clear transparent margin within its own cell. Animation left to right. THREE frames: 1 narrow black-violet steel shadow nail descending diagonally with thin violet band, 2 nail pins a little black ground shadow, short purple chain appears from nail to shadow, 3 pinned shadow held by a thin glowing violet band and tiny white spark. Compact transparent isometric ground effect.
