# bone-stake

Built-in image_gen. Transparent RGBA preserved, common registration and nearest-neighbor sampling.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: RPG game VFX spritesheet. Crisp detailed pixel art with hard pixel clusters, teal source magic, black violet curses and luminous ivory bone impacts. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no checkerboard painted, no text, labels, grid, ground, character or borders. Equal-width horizontal cells in ONE row. Each frame centered in its own cell with at least 8% empty margin all sides. Same framing/scale across frames. Exactly 3 animation frames. A tall jagged bone curse stake erupting vertically out of the ground. Three frames: short emerging bone tip; half height with purple rune cracks; full tall carved ivory spire teal inner energy and violet sparks. Complete tip/base inside each cell.
