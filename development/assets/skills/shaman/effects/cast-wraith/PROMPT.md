# cast-wraith

Built-in image_gen. Transparent RGBA preserved, common registration and nearest-neighbor sampling.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Derive a new TRANSPARENT pixel-art RPG VFX spritesheet from the attached spectral skull magic reference. Preserve its ivory skulls, brilliant mint-teal edges, violet spirit smoke and crisp pixel clusters; replace the circular composition with UPRISING WRAITHS around a caster's whole body. Exactly FOUR frames, one row, four equal square cells. No character is drawn; keep the entire central 30% wide column mostly transparent so the caster remains readable. Shared scale and ground baseline at local y=.85 in EVERY cell; 12% empty transparent margins on all sides, nothing clipped. Frame1 two small spectral ivory faces at (.25,.68),(.75,.68), long thin tapering tails attached to ground at (.25,.85),(.75,.85). Frame2 faces rise to (.24,.49),(.76,.42), elongated wispy spirit bodies reaching back to same ground. Frame3 faces rise to (.24,.27),(.76,.21), open mouths, arms as curved spirit ribbons up the two sides, strong ivory/mint edge highlights with translucent violet interiors. Frame4 faces drift to (.23,.19),(.77,.15) and dissolve into upward streaks, tails still terminate near ground. Maintain recognizable skulls and two side wraiths, no huge opaque clouds. It should form a tall U shape of spirits with empty center, for a shaman casting an early spell at 190 px body height. No text, border, labels, grid, floor or painted checkerboard. Actual alpha transparency.
