# dot-wraith

Built-in image_gen. Transparent RGBA preserved, common registration and nearest-neighbor sampling.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Take ONE of the upright individual skull-wraiths in this reference and create its THREE-frame loop as a transparent pixel-art spritesheet. Remove every other face and every other wraith: exactly ONE skull face and ONE tapered upright spirit body in each frame. Not a circular swarm. Preserve the brilliant mint edges, ivory skull and violet flame pixel-art style. Exactly THREE equal-width square horizontal cells, one row, shared scale and ground baseline local (.50,.84). The entire specter fits inside x=.20..80 and y=.18..84 in every frame, including all smoke tips. Leave at least 15% empty transparent margin above the tallest tip and generous transparent space left/right. Frame1 skull (.50,.38), tail anchored (.50,.84), arms curl at (.26,.52)/(.74,.52). Frame2 skull (.50,.31), tail samepoint, arms open to (.23,.47)/(.77,.47), head rising slightly. Frame3 skull (.50,.35), tail samepoint, arms return toward firstpose. Skull mouth open, hollow mostly transparent midbody, thin bright outer ribbons, readable silhouette. No secondary skulls, ring of heads, character/enemy, weapons, floor, labels, text, grid, shadows or checkerboard painting. Actual alpha background.
