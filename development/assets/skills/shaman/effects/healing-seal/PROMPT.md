Each frame centered in its own cell with at least 8% empty margin all sides. Same framing/scale across frames. Exactly 3 animation frames. A small emerald-green life ring with a diagonal violet bone seal shutting it: green ring; purple slash crosses; sealed ring flares with bone fragments. Simple readable curse graphic.
