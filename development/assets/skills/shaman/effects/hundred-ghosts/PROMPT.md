# hundred-ghosts

Built-in image_gen. Transparent RGBA preserved, common registration and nearest-neighbor sampling.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: RPG game VFX spritesheet. Crisp detailed pixel art with hard pixel clusters, teal source magic, black violet curses and luminous ivory bone impacts. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no checkerboard painted, no text, labels, grid, ground, character or borders. Equal-width horizontal cells in ONE row. Each frame centered in its own cell with at least 8% empty margin all sides. Same framing/scale across frames. Exactly 4 animation frames. Ultimate ritual spectral ghost skull swarm as one animated VFX asset, transparent. Four frames: a crescent of small ivory skull-shaped spirits orbit clockwise; broad orbit with violet tails; spirits thrust inward; orbit reset with teal sparks. Hollow middle; many small distinguishable spirits, no solid smoke cloud.
