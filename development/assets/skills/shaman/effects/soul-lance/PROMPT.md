Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: RPG game VFX spritesheet. Crisp detailed pixel art with hard pixel clusters, teal source magic, black violet curses and luminous ivory bone impacts. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no checkerboard painted, no text, labels, grid, ground, character or borders. Equal-width horizontal cells in ONE row. Each frame centered in its own cell with at least 8% empty margin all sides. Same framing/scale across frames. Exactly 3 animation frames. A rightward bone-white spectral lance with teal core piercing a cracked skull rune. Three frames: thin ghost lance; brilliant needle plus fractured violet skull halo; dissipating ivory fragments. No weapon held by human.
